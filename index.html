How misses work: there is no time limit and you get one scored try. After a miss you see the answer and tap it yourself to continue, and that card comes back a few cards later. Use “Show me” when you don't know instead of guessing.

Smart order favors cards you miss or answer slowly. Spelling is strict: F♯maj7 needs E♯, and an enharmonic answer like F counts as a miss.
